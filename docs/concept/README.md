# Concept art

Reference images for the game's look. **Nothing in this folder ships in the game.**

| File | What it is | Made with |
|---|---|---|
| `deserter-sheet.png` | The Deserter: front, side and back views plus an 8-colour palette | AI-generated (ChatGPT) from a prompt by Georgi |

## How concept art is used

1. A concept sheet fixes a character's silhouette and palette.
2. The in-game sprite is drawn by hand at its real size (16×24 px for player characters), simplifying every detail to a few pixels. Shrinking a concept image automatically turns it to mud.
3. AI-generated images stay here as references only. Steam requires disclosure of AI-generated content that appears in the game, on the store page or in marketing.

## The Deserter's palette

Taken from `deserter-sheet.png`, plus the skin tones, cloak shadow and highlight the sheet was missing. The skin is darker and weathered on purpose: the first sprite read as a young, innocent kid, not a soldier who fled a war.
